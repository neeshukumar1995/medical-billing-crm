Trust & Security

Access control, end to end

  • ✓Permission guards on every page — a user without the right permission never sees the screen, not just the button.
  • ✓Client-status aware login — Inactive or Suspended clients are blocked at sign-in with a clear message.
  • ✓Scoped data access — users only see the client (or clients) they've been explicitly granted.
